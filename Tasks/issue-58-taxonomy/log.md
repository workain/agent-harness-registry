## 5. Mutation test — the spec's `related_components:` link is INERT on a bundle

§ 8.0 mandates `related_components: [base-project-template]` on the new bundle YAML, and the
acceptance criterion says both link fields must "resolve in both directions". Before trusting
that, I read `scripts/generate.py:523-527` and then **proved the behaviour by mutation** rather
than by reading:

```

EXIT=1
```

Both mutations reverted; tree back to clean.

**Finding.** `_check_cross_links` is invoked three times (`generate.py:525-527`): on
`(components + bundles).related_research`, on `(research).related_components`, and on
`(research).related_research`. **`related_components:` on a component or bundle is checked by
nothing** — a garbage slug passes silently — **and is rendered by nothing**, because
`_relevant_components_cell` is only called from `render_research_table`.

So the field § 8.0 asks for is real YAML the catalog carries but the generator ignores in both
respects. This is wider than the caveat in my brief (which said only that the linter does not
*create* the back-link).

**Resolution — do both, and say which is which.** I set the spec-mandated
`related_components:` on *both* sides by hand (it is the documented intent and costs nothing,
it is simply inert today), **and** additionally wire the `related_research` ⇄
`related_components` direction through `data/research/base-project-template-evidence.yaml`,
which is the only path `generate.py` both checks and renders. That is what makes the third
acceptance criterion mechanically demonstrable instead of merely asserted. Flagged to the epic
rather than treated as closed by me.
